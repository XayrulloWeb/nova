Frequently
Asked

Is prior coding experience required?

add

While a basic understanding of logic is helpful, Kinetic Noir is designed for those with high design sensibilities regardless of technical background. We start from foundational principles and rapidly scale into advanced architecture.

What is the payment structure?

add

We operate on a lab-fee model. Tuition is paid per module, allowing for flexible progression. We also offer select Income Share Agreements for exceptional portfolios.

Is the curriculum remote or in-person?

add

Kinetic Noir is a hybrid experience. Core lectures and crits occur in our virtual gallery spaces (spatial web), while select intensive workshops are held in physical labs in key cities.

What kind of hardware do I need?

add

A modern machine capable of rendering WebGL/Three.js environments smoothly is required. Detailed specs are provided upon admission, but generally, a recent M-series Mac or equivalent PC is sufficient.
